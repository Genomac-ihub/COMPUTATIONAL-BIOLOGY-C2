**📚 Overview**  
Data visualization is more than making charts — it's about telling the story hidden in the data.  
   
 In genomics, visual tools can help you:  
- Identify mutation patterns and frequencies  
- Explore sequencing coverage maps  
- Detect trends and anomalies in large datasets  
- Communicate complex findings clearly to collaborators  
We will focus on **practical, reproducible visualization techniques** that enable hypothesis generation and data-driven decision making.
