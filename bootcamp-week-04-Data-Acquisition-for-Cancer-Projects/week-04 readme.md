**📚 Overview**  
In this module, we explore practical strategies for retrieving and combining different types of cancer-related data.  
**🔍 Key Topics Covered**  
- **Data Acquisition Methods**:  
- Copy Number Variation (CNV) data  
- RNA-Sequencing (RNA-Seq) data  
- Clinical data (e.g., patient demographics, diagnosis, treatment outcomes)  
- Proteomics data  
- **Data Collection Approaches**:  
1. **Field Research (In-House Data Collection)**  
- ✅ Custom and controlled, but often expensive and time-consuming  
- ❌ Limited scale and may lack standardization  
2. **Requesting Controlled/Private Data**  
- ✅ High-quality and standardized  
- ❌ Access restrictions and long approval processes  
3. **Using Publicly Available Databases**  
- ✅ Freely accessible and large-scale  
- ❌ May require extensive preprocessing and validation  
- **Whole Data Integration**:  
   
 Learn how merging diverse data types enables a more **comprehensive view of biological systems** and supports robust  **multi-dimensional analysis**.

**💡 Learning Objectives**  
By the end of this module, you should be able to:  
- Identify and access cancer genomics data from multiple sources  
- Compare various data acquisition strategies, including their trade-offs  
- Understand how to merge heterogeneous data types such as:  
- Clinical  
- CNV  
- RNA-Seq  
- Proteomics  
- Recognize the value of integrating data for a **whole data perspective** in cancer research
