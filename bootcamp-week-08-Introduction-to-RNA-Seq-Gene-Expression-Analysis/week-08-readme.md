# 🧬 Week 8: Introduction to RNA-Seq & Gene Expression Analysis

Welcome to **Week 8** of the Computational Biology Bootcamp!  
This week we dive into one of the most powerful and widely used techniques in modern genomics — **RNA sequencing (RNA-Seq)** — and explore how it connects with **gene expression** and **proteomics** to deepen our understanding of diseases like cancer.

---

## 📚 Overview

### 🧪 Topics Covered:

- Fundamentals of **RNA-Seq** and how it is used to measure gene expression
- The concept of **differential gene expression**
- Basics of **proteomics** and how it complements transcriptomic data

### 🌐 Why It Matters:

- RNA-Seq is considered the **gold standard** in **transcriptomics** — helping researchers understand what genes are actively expressed in different conditions
- **Proteomics** provides insights at the protein level — revealing how gene expression translates into functional molecules
- Together, RNA-Seq and proteomics enable **multi-dimensional analysis** in cancer biology and precision medicine

---

## 💡 Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

- Describe the **RNA-Seq workflow**, from sequencing to quantification
- Understand methods for **gene expression normalization**
- Interpret the meaning of **differential expression** results
- Recognize how **transcriptomic and proteomic** data provide a fuller picture of genomic activity
